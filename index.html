WHAT THAT ADDS UP TO

I care less about producing tests than producing useful confidence.

01

Test where the truth lives.

A screen saying “success” is not always the truth. Some risks belong in the browser, some at the API, some in the database, and some on the device itself.

02

Make failures useful.

A failing test should leave enough evidence to understand what happened. I work on deterministic data, logs, screenshots, traces and failure paths so debugging starts with information instead of guessing.

03

Stay close to the product.

I do not stop at “the test found a bug.” I am comfortable reading and changing frontend, backend, data and build code when that is what it takes to understand and fix the real problem.

SAME HABIT, NEW TERRITORY

I’m also applying the same QA mindset to AI and agent tooling.

This is an extension of my quality work, not a change of profession. I look for familiar problems in a newer system: what was actually evaluated, whether missing information can look like success, and whether a failure can be reproduced and checked again.
