How I work

A green test matters only when I understand why it is green.

Make it reproducible first

Control test data and environment state, reproduce the failure, then decide what to fix. A lucky rerun is not evidence.

Check the real source of truth

A success message on screen may not mean the backend or database succeeded. I verify API, DB, or rendered truth when that is what actually matters.

Separate product bugs from test bugs

Tests can drift and products can break. I identify which one failed before changing timeouts, retries, or application code.
