CommerceOps Support

Support for the CommerceOps iOS practice and simulation application.

CommerceOps is a training application. Purchases, payments, refunds, inventory changes, and staff workflows in the app are simulations and do not affect real stores, real orders, or real payment accounts.

Contact support

Email x3434390@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include the screen or workflow you were using and what you expected to happen. Screenshots are welcome when they do not contain sensitive information.

Please do not send sensitive information

Do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, CVC values, government identifiers, or other sensitive personal information. CommerceOps does not need real payment credentials for support or for its simulated checkout.

Camera and location

Camera and location access are optional. If either permission is unavailable or declined, CommerceOps provides a manual alternative: barcode entry for item lookup and city/address search for practice stores.

Local app data

The App Store version keeps its practice workflow state on your device. If a practice scenario becomes inconsistent, use the app's reset function to restore the bundled deterministic starting state. Removing the app also clears its local application storage according to iOS behavior.

Privacy

See the CommerceOps Privacy Policy for details about local storage, permissions, simulated payments, and support email.
