CommerceOps Privacy Policy

Effective: September 3, 2026

CommerceOps is a training and workflow-simulation application. The iOS App Store version is designed to run its commerce practice workflows locally on your device and does not require a CommerceOps production account or remote CommerceOps backend.

Information stored by the app

CommerceOps stores practice state locally on your device so that simulated workflows can continue between sessions. This can include the selected demo role/session, cart and order state, simulated returns and refunds, inventory and fulfillment changes, reward and store selections, manually entered store-search text, locally submitted practice feedback, and app-generated audit/event history.

The app includes bundled demo accounts and synthetic fixture information for training. These are not real customer accounts. CommerceOps does not provide public account registration in the iOS 1.0 release.

CommerceOps Pro subscription

CommerceOps Pro is an optional auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the purchase, billing account, payment method, renewal, cancellation, and App Store transaction infrastructure.

The app uses Apple's StoreKit framework on the device to load the subscription product and localized price, initiate purchases or restores, and verify whether the Apple Account currently has an active CommerceOps Pro entitlement. That entitlement status is used only to unlock the Support, Supervisor, and Operations training workspaces and advanced multi-role scenarios. CommerceOps does not operate a billing server for the iOS 1.0 release and does not receive or store your full App Store payment-card information.

Apple may process purchase, account, device, and payment information under Apple's own privacy terms and App Store policies.

Payment simulation inside training workflows

Commerce workflow checkout is simulated and is separate from the real CommerceOps Pro App Store subscription. The simulated checkout form may display fields for a cardholder name, card number, expiry date, and CVC, but CommerceOps does not read, save, or transmit the full values entered into those checkout fields. Completing the simulated payment records only a fixed practice payment result.

The separate practice payment-method feature can store a user-entered card label, card brand, last four digits, and expiry value locally on the device. Do not enter real payment credentials into CommerceOps. Full card numbers and CVC values are not required for saved practice payment methods.

Camera

Camera access is optional and is requested only when you choose the camera action on the item-scan screen. In the iOS 1.0 release, the camera is used for an on-screen preview. CommerceOps does not upload or retain camera images or video. Manual barcode entry remains available without camera access.

Location

Location access is optional and is requested only when you choose to use your current location while browsing practice stores. CommerceOps does not retain or transmit your latitude or longitude. The app records only the resulting local permission state for the practice workflow and uses a simulated local search result. Manual city/address search remains available without location access.

Network access, analytics, advertising, and tracking

The CommerceOps iOS 1.0 practice runtime does not send its commerce workflow state to a CommerceOps server. It does not include first-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or an analytics service in the shipped application runtime.

Network access can occur when the app communicates with Apple's App Store services for CommerceOps Pro product information, purchase, restore, and entitlement verification, and when you open public CommerceOps privacy/support pages. CommerceOps does not add a first-party analytics or advertising layer to those interactions.

If you open this privacy page or the CommerceOps support page, those public pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process ordinary web-request information such as IP address, browser/device information, and service logs under GitHub's own privacy terms. CommerceOps does not add analytics or advertising scripts to these pages.

Support email

If you contact CommerceOps Support by email, your email provider and Google's Gmail service will process the address, message content, attachments, and related delivery metadata needed to send and receive that message. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card information, or other sensitive information. Support correspondence is used only to respond to the request and can be deleted on request when it is no longer required for the support conversation.

Retention and deletion

Local practice state remains on the device until it is reset or the app's local storage is cleared, such as by removing the app. The app's reset function restores the bundled deterministic practice state.

CommerceOps Pro subscription and transaction records are managed by Apple according to Apple's account, billing, legal, and retention requirements. Canceling a subscription stops future renewal but does not necessarily delete Apple's historical transaction records.

For support emails, you may request correction or deletion of information you sent by contacting the address below. Requests concerning data held by third-party services are also subject to those services' own policies and legal obligations.

Your choices

You can decline camera or location permission and continue using the corresponding manual alternatives. You can later review or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings.

You can use the customer-side CommerceOps training workflow without CommerceOps Pro. If you subscribe to Pro, you can manage or cancel the subscription through Apple's subscription settings. Pro access normally continues until the end of the current paid entitlement period after cancellation.
